3. The Challenge
This Contest involves the following challenge:
Teams participating in the India Agentic AI Open Hackathon may select only any 1 (one) of the
following track :

1. Track A: Agentic Workflows This track involves the development of multi-agent workflows and
services for practical applications utilizing the NVIDIA agentic AI stack, such as enterprise
workflow automation agents.

2. Track B: Model Finetuning and Customisation: This track is centered on the training, fine-
tuning, and optimisation of models using the NVIDIA NeMo & Nemotron suite of software and

models, including domain-specific models for sectors like finance, healthcare, telco, legal, or
manufacturing.
3. Track C: SDG (Synthetic Data Generation): The objective of this track is the creation of
pipelines for generating high-quality training data, such as synthetic multilingual conversation
datasets.
